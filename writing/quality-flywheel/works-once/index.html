“Works Once” Has Two Opposites

An assistant that can succeed and an assistant you can leave unattended require different evidence.

Atlas finally diagnoses the expired certificate correctly. The team has a successful example. How much confidence should that buy?

The answer depends on the product. A developer who generates five candidate patches and runs tests can benefit from one good attempt. An unattended operator that must carry out five successive jobs needs reliable execution every time. Best-of-many capability and repeated dependability reward different behavior.

The first probability is 1 − (1 − p)k; the second is pk. Real tasks have different difficulties and attempts may be correlated, so this table is a conceptual illustration. It also assumes someone can recognize the successful attempt. Generating five answers does not automatically select the correct one.

τ-bench evaluates tool-using agents through final database state and introduces passk to examine consistency across trials. It puts a product question into the metric: how dependable is this behavior over repeated attempts? [21]

There is another hidden distinction: a correct endpoint can conceal a defective path. Atlas might eventually identify the certificate after issuing an unauthorized restart. An answer-only grader sees success; the user experiences an avoidable action.

The September 2026 Traverse/Scout preprint studies hidden failures across software engineering, computer use, and science trajectories. It reports that even the strongest of six tested frontier judges locates the first mistake in fewer than one-third of runs. Its trained verifier improves localization and selection among candidate runs. That selection consumes multiple attempts; it should not be described as a free improvement in single-attempt capability. [22]

Finally, keep the world stable enough to compare. If the original certificate incident later becomes a network outage, the old answer key may no longer describe the task. Record relevant state, validate the reference, and declare replay's limits. Unsupported tool requests should appear as missing coverage, rather than receive invented plausible answers.

THE POWERFUL IDEA

Autonomy raises the value of consistency, process integrity, and a faithful environment.

Atlas is ready for more responsibility when the evidence matches that responsibility. One compelling example establishes possibility. Repeated, representative, independently checked performance supports a much stronger claim.
